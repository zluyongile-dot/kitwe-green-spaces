Goal & Urban Forestry Context

Why comparative spatial evaluation matters to Copperbelt municipalities.

The Spatial Green Equity Problem

Copperbelt mining and industrial growth have caused rapid urbanization, leading to significant fragmentation of tree cover and unequal access to municipal green spaces.

  • Kitwe features massive tailing dumps that require planned forestry reclamation.
  • Peripheral high-density settlements suffer a distinct lack of accessible shade parks.
  • Stakeholders need interactive GIS maps to identify coverage gaps and target capital investments.
Multi-City Benchmarking

Integrating Ndola (benchmark reference) with Kitwe (primary focus) establishes a standard to compare canopy density, accessibility indexes, and ecological indicators between neighboring industrial districts.

Full-Stack GIS System Architecture

Preserving 100% offline capabilities with Postgres/PostGIS and JS fallback buffers.

Full-Stack Components
  • Spatial Database: PostgreSQL with PostGIS Extension for coordinate projections (WGS84, SRID 4326).
  • RESTful Backend API: Python Flask server exposing dynamically generated GeoJSON and weather polling routes.
  • Offline Parity Layer: Embedded Javascript fallback caches (`static-data.js` & `fallback-data.js`) trigger automatically to guarantee functionality without an active database connection.

Comparative Insights

Kitwe maintains a larger total woodland profile, but Ndola's central parks average a higher NDVI density score (average NDVI: 0.54 vs 0.48). Neither city currently satisfies the WHO recommended target of 9 m² of green space per capita, showcasing a clear developmental gap.

Live Environmental Telemetry Feeds

Real-time weather parameters and air quality indicators polled directly via REST APIs.

Live Sensor Telemetry APIs

The system links geographical coordinate arrays to active telemetry sensors, allowing users to select individual stations or compare both districts simultaneously.

  • Open-Meteo API: Polls hourly microclimatic temperatures and generates double-curve line charts for temperature comparison.
  • WAQI Sensor API: Extracts active PM2.5 and PM10 particulate levels to compute local air quality.
  • Clustered Visualizations: Compiles telemetry side-by-side using high-performance Chart.js canvases.

Interactive Simulation & Report Generation

Empowering stakeholders with town simulator models and PDF publications.

Planner Simulator

Users can tap anywhere on the map to draft a proposed green space. The simulator uses Turf.js to compute carbon sequestration capacity, stormwater retention index, walksheds, and cooling radius offsets in real-time.

PDF Publications

Overhauled with modern styling and client-side calculations. The compiler generates comprehensive, formatted, multi-page PDFs with study-boundary page borders. This provides stakeholders with immediate, printable capstone data sheets.
