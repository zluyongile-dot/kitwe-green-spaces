Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the green space data?
Data is sourced from high-res satellite imagery, validated with ground surveys, achieving 95%+ accuracy across 16 wards.
Can I contribute or report an issue?
Yes, citizens can submit reports through the interactive map. Our team verifies and updates the platform weekly.
Is the platform free for researchers?
Absolutely. Academic and non-profit use is free with attribution. Contact us for API access.
How often is data updated?
Quarterly updates with real-time citizen reports. Major changes are reflected within 2 weeks.
